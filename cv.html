Selected projects

CO₂ Utilization for Green Methanol

Fourth-Year Plant Design · Kathmandu University

Team conceptual design and Aspen Plus V15 simulation of a power-to-methanol process in Nepal. The integrated flowsheet covers MEA-based CO2 capture from cement-kiln flue gas, alkaline water electrolysis for green hydrogen, reverse water-gas shift, a methanol synthesis loop with recycle, and two-stage distillation. Project scope also includes process flow and piping and instrumentation diagrams, mass and energy balances, equipment and instrumentation specification, and techno-economic assessment. The study also considered cost sensitivities and process-improvement options, including hydrogen recovery from purge gas, staged RWGS with water removal, and heat integration.
