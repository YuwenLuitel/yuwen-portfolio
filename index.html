01Fourth-Year Plant Design ProjectKathmandu University

CO₂ utilization for green methanol

Contributed to a team plant-design study and Aspen Plus simulation of a CO₂-to-methanol process integrating cement-flue-gas capture, alkaline electrolysis, reverse water-gas shift, methanol synthesis, recycle, and two-stage distillation. The study also covered equipment and instrumentation, process diagrams, mass and energy balances, and techno-economic evaluation.
